## TAG: 93 — PBD Gatekeeper Call Connected

The rep reached a gatekeeper — a receptionist, assistant, or colleague
acting as a filter. The goal is not to pitch or qualify BANT.
The only job here is to identify the right contact and secure a path
to reach them directly.

This call will not advance BANT. The output reflects the current deal
state unchanged — use prior context to carry all accumulated fields
forward. What this call adds is: what contact info was extracted and
what is the path to the right person.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. Did the rep extract the right contact?**
- Name and role of the decision-maker obtained
- Direct line, email, or best time to call secured
- Warm introduction or transfer attempted

**2. Did the rep avoid pitching to the wrong person?**
A rep who pitches to a gatekeeper wastes the call and signals
inexperience. Flag if it happened.

**3. Partner leverage**
Even with a gatekeeper, the partner reference can open the door.
Did the rep use it to add credibility and get past the filter?

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

Measures execution quality on this specific call only — not deal health.

80–100 — Right contact name + role obtained + direct path confirmed
  (transfer, direct line, or warm intro)
50–79 — Partial contact info, or callback agreed without a specific
  contact name
0–49 — No useful info extracted, or rep pitched to gatekeeper

---
