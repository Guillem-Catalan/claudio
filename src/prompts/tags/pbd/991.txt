## TAG: 991 — PBD Partner Call

This is a coordination call between the PBD rep and a partner contact
(Banco Santander, Telefónica, or MEO). No prospect is on the line.
The goal is to align on joint pipeline: which prospects to activate,
which deals need partner support, and what concrete actions each party
will take.

Do NOT evaluate BANT or script compliance as prospect-facing frameworks.
Evaluate partner relationship management quality instead.

If deal context is available (prior calls and audits from this deal),
use it to evaluate whether this coordination call is actually advancing
that specific deal — or whether the rep is coordinating without effect.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. Deals discussed**
Which specific prospects or deals were mentioned?
For each: what is the current status, what was said, what was agreed?

**2. Partner activation quality**
Is the rep treating the partner as a passive info source,
or actively directing them to open doors, make introductions,
send warm referrals, or unblock deals?
A rep who only asks "any news?" scores low.
A rep who says "I need you to call Cliente X before Friday
to confirm they received my email" scores high.

**3. Actions agreed**
Were concrete next actions committed to by both sides?
- Strong: specific action + named party + deadline
- Weak: vague agreement ("I'll look into it", "we'll see")
- None: call ended without clear commitments

**4. Intel value**
Did the rep extract new, useful information about any prospect
that could be used to advance a deal?
(e.g., prospect's decision timeline, internal resistance,
budget status, who to contact next)

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

Measure of how effectively the rep is managing the partner relationship
and advancing joint pipeline through this call.

80–100: Specific deals discussed + partner directed to take concrete actions
  + new intel obtained + clear next steps with dates
50–79: Some deals discussed + partial actions agreed + limited intel
0–49: No specific deals advanced + vague coordination + no clear output

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return a single JSON object. All fields required.
BANT fields: all "Missing" with null evidence (no prospect on the line).
Script compliance: all "PASS" (not applicable — do not penalize).

{
  "win_rate_score": integer (0–100),
  "forecast_flag": "GREEN" | "AMBER" | "RED",
  "partner_leverage_score": integer (1–4),
  "lead_temperature": "Warm",

    "budget":    { "status": "Missing", "evidence": null },

  },

  },
  "objections": null,
  "buying_signals": null,
  "blockers": "string or null",

deal_context: Summary of which deals were discussed, what the partner
said about each, and what actions were agreed. If prior deal context
is available, indicate whether this call advanced or stalled that deal.

biggest_gap: The main reason this call did not advance deals as much
as it could have — from the rep's side.

next_call_objective: What the rep should align with the partner on
in the next coordination call.

next_action_rep: The most important thing the rep must do before
the next partner call — specific, not generic.

tl_note: Flag if the rep is under-utilizing the partner, not following
up on agreed actions, or if a deal is at risk. null if well managed.
