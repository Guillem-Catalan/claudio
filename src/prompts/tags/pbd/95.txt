## TAG: 95 — PBD Connected Call - Busy/Bad Time

The prospect picked up but couldn't talk. The only job here is to
handle the interruption cleanly and secure a concrete callback with
a specific date and time — not to pitch.

This call does not advance BANT. Carry all accumulated deal state
forward unchanged. What this call adds is whether the rep secured
a real callback or left it open-ended.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. Callback quality**
- Did the rep secure a specific date and time for the callback?
- Or did they leave it vague ("I'll call you back", "whenever works")?
- Did they confirm before hanging up?

**2. Did the rep avoid pitching?**
A rep who starts pitching when the prospect says "bad time" loses
credibility and risks losing the callback. Flag if it happened.

**3. Partner leverage**
A quick reference to the partner before agreeing to call back adds
context and increases the chance the prospect picks up next time.

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

Measures execution quality on this specific call only.

80–100 — Callback secured with specific date + time confirmed
50–79 — Callback agreed but vague date or time
0–49 — No callback secured, or rep pitched when told it was a bad time
