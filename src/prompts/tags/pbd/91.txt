## TAG: 91 — PBD Demo Scheduled

This call ended with a demo booked. Evaluate the quality of the booking
and the state of the deal at this moment — not just the call in isolation.

Use all prior context provided (previous calls, emails, deal history)
to assess how BANT has accumulated across the deal, not just this call.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. BANT at booking (cumulative)**
Evaluate BANT as it stands at this moment across the full deal.
A pillar confirmed in a previous call remains Confirmed unless
directly contradicted. A pillar Missing across all calls is a real gap.

**2. Demo booking quality**
Was the booking strong or weak?
- Strong: specific date + time + participants confirmed + reconfirmation
  technique used (e.g., calendar link sent, prospect verbally confirmed)
- Weak: vague "let's find a time" or "I'll send a calendar link"
- Prospect-initiated: prospect suggests the demo themselves —
  the rep didn't close, the prospect volunteered

**3. Partner leverage (cumulative)**
Did the rep consistently use the partner relationship throughout the
full PBD process to open doors, reduce resistance, and create urgency?
Evaluate the pattern across all interactions — not just this call.

**4. Objections across the deal**
What objections came up at any point? Were they handled or bypassed?
An objection bypassed (not handled) is a risk that will resurface
in the PAE demo.

**5. Handover readiness**
Is there enough information in this deal for a PAE to walk into
a demo with a clear picture of who they're meeting, why they
accepted, and what matters to them?

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

80–100 — BANT 3–4 confirmed + strong close (date/time/participants
  confirmed) + partner leveraged consistently across the deal +
  objections handled or absent
50–79 — BANT 2 confirmed + decent close OR partner underused across
  the deal OR one unhandled objection
0–49 — BANT 0–1 OR demo booked by prospect initiative OR no
  date/time confirmed + objections bypassed

---
