## TAG: 92 — PBD Positive Champion Connected Call

The rep connected with the right person — the champion or decision-maker —
but did not book a demo. This is a critical PBD stage: BANT starts
accumulating here and the deal either builds real momentum or stalls.

Use all prior context (previous calls, emails, deal history) to evaluate
how this call advanced the deal, what was qualified, and whether the rep
created a credible path to a demo.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. BANT progress (cumulative)**
What did this call add to the deal's BANT state?
Evaluate BANT as it stands now across the full deal — not just this call.
A call where no new BANT was qualified is a wasted connection.

**2. Why was no demo booked?**
Identify the primary reason and whether it was justified:
- Rep did not attempt to propose a demo
- Rep proposed it but didn't push for confirmation (weak close)
- Rep stayed in discovery or pitch mode — never transitioned to commitment
- Prospect not interested or explicitly refused
- Timing constraint (no agenda available, asked to follow up later)
- Insufficient qualification — not enough context to justify a demo yet
- Other (specify)

**3. Next step quality**
Was any concrete next step secured?
- Strong: specific date + time confirmed by prospect
- Weak: open-ended ("I'll send you info", "call me next week")
- None: conversation ended without any agreed next step

**4. Champion quality**
Is the person reached the right contact?
- Decision-maker: signs or directly approves
- Influencer: has access to decision-maker, can advocate internally
- Gatekeeper: no real role in the decision

**5. Partner leverage (cumulative)**
Did the rep use the partner relationship throughout the full process
so far — to open doors, reduce resistance, and build trust?
Evaluate the pattern across all interactions, not just this call.

**6. Script execution (cumulative)**
Evaluate whether each script phase has been completed across the full
process. If the opener was done correctly in a previous call, it is done.

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

80–100 — Correct champion reached + BANT 2+ pillars confirmed
  (cumulative) + clear attempt to book demo + concrete next step
  with date confirmed. OR valid reason demo wasn't possible yet
  (timing, internal check needed) with a strong agreed follow-up.
50–79 — Champion reached + some BANT progress + attempt to book demo
  but weak transition or vague next step.
0–49 — No meaningful BANT progress OR no demo attempt OR no next step
  OR wrong contact reached.

---
