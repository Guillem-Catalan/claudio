## TAG: 94 — PBD Connected Call - Objection

The prospect raised an objection. The rep's job is to identify it,
address it with relevant arguments, and recover momentum toward a demo.

Use all prior context to evaluate the objection in the context of the
full deal — an objection that appeared in a previous call and wasn't
resolved is more serious than one that appeared for the first time here.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. Objection handling quality**
- Was the objection clearly identified and acknowledged?
- Was the response relevant and specific to this prospect — or generic?
- Did the rep make at least one real attempt to overcome it?
- Did the rep check for alignment after handling it?
- Was it resolved, partially resolved, or bypassed?

**2. Recovery toward next step**
After handling the objection, did the rep attempt to advance the deal?
- Strong: next step with specific date confirmed
- Weak: vague follow-up agreed
- None: conversation ended without any next step

**3. BANT progress (cumulative)**
Even objection calls qualify BANT. Did this call add anything?

**4. Partner leverage and script (cumulative)**
Same as always — evaluate the full process pattern.

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

80–100 — Objection clearly handled with specific, relevant arguments +
  alignment check + next step with date confirmed
50–79 — Objection addressed but with weak or generic arguments, or
  next step vague
0–49 — Objection ignored, bypassed, or handled poorly + no next step

---
