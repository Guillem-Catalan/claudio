## TAG: 99 — PBD Connected Call - Wrong Champion/Person

The rep reached someone at the company but not the right contact —
a colleague or employee, not a gatekeeper. This person is not a filter;
they simply are not the decision-maker.

The goal is to extract the right contact's name and role, and ideally
get a warm introduction or direct transfer.

This call does not advance BANT unless the right contact's identity
is explicitly confirmed. Carry all accumulated deal state forward.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. Did the rep extract the right contact?**
- Name and role of the decision-maker obtained
- Direct line, email, or warm intro secured
- Did the rep leverage this person to get closer to the right contact?

**2. Did the rep avoid pitching to the wrong person?**
Pitching to someone who has no decision power wastes the call.
Flag if it happened.

**3. Partner leverage**
The partner reference adds credibility even with the wrong person
and increases the chance of getting a warm intro.

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

Measures execution quality on this specific call only.

80–100 — Right contact name + role confirmed + warm intro or direct
  path secured
50–79 — Partial info obtained — name but no role, or role but no name
0–49 — No useful contact info extracted, or rep pitched to wrong person
