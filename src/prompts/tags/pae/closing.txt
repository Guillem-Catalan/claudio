## TAG: PAE Closing Call / PAE Closing Meeting

An explicit close attempt has occurred. Use this call plus the full
deal history to produce a current snapshot of where this deal stands.

Your job is not to audit this call in isolation. It is to answer:
given everything that has happened — PBD qualification, demo(s),
follow-ups, emails — is this deal going to close, when, and what
is in the way?

This call is the most recent update to that picture.

---

## WHAT TO ASSESS

**Close readiness**
Is this deal actually ready to close, or is the rep forcing a close
on a deal that isn't there yet?
- Is MEDDIC 5-6/6? A close attempt with major pillars Missing is premature.
- Is the economic buyer engaged and aligned on price?
- Is there a specific signature date on the table — not "end of quarter"?
- Are decision criteria met — does Factorial check every mandatory box?

**What this call resolved or exposed**
Did the close attempt surface hidden blockers — a new stakeholder,
a budget freeze, a competing vendor? Or did it confirm the deal is real?

**Closing technique**
Did the rep create urgency and ask for the signature directly?
Or did they leave the door open for more delay?
A rep who says "take your time" on a closing call is not closing.

**Partner leverage (cumulative)**
Has the partner been used as a final accelerator — partner deadlines,
partner contact involved to unblock, exclusive pricing framing?

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

Not a score of this call. The current probability of closing this deal.
Based on everything known: MEDDIC, economic buyer alignment,
price agreed, signature timeline, and whether blockers are real or excuses.

80–100 — MEDDIC 5-6/6 + economic buyer aligned + price agreed +
  specific signature date + no critical blockers
50–79 — MEDDIC 4/6 + price in range but not locked + timeline
  "soon" but not specific
0–49 — MEDDIC 0–3 OR economic buyer not engaged OR price not agreed
  OR deal has fundamental unresolved blockers
