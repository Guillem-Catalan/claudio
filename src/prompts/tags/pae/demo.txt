## TAG: PAE Demo

The PAE runs a demo. This may be the first demo or a subsequent one
with additional stakeholders — the deal context will make this clear.
Evaluate accordingly: a second demo with 2/6 MEDDIC is a worse signal
than a first demo with 2/6.

Use all prior context: PBD calls, emails, handover brief, and any
previous PAE interactions. Evaluate not just this call but whether
the PAE used the context available to them.

---

## WHAT TO EVALUATE

**1. MEDDIC coverage for this stage**
Evaluate relative to how many demos have already occurred:
- Were Metrics quantified — or did the PAE leave impact as generic?
- Was the Economic Buyer identified? Is it the person in the room?
- Were Decision Criteria surfaced — what does the prospect need to say yes?
- Was Decision Process mapped — who else is involved, in what order?
- Is there a Champion emerging? Is this person engaged and capable
  of defending Factorial internally?
- Was Competition addressed — is the prospect evaluating alternatives?

**2. Discovery quality**
Did the PAE run real discovery or jump straight into the product?
Real discovery = specific pains verbalized by the prospect, business
impact understood, context used to tailor the demo.
Generic discovery = "what are your main HR challenges?" with no follow-up.

**3. Did the PAE use the prior context?**
The handover and prior call history exist. Did the PAE reference them —
the prospect's pain, the objections raised, what motivated the demo?
A PAE who ignores prior context is starting from zero unnecessarily.
If this is a second demo with new stakeholders: did the PAE arrive
knowing what each new stakeholder cares about and prepare accordingly?

**4. Partner leverage (cumulative)**
Has the partner relationship been used across the full process?
In the demo context: was the partner discount and trust used to
reduce adoption risk and accelerate the decision?

**5. Demo outcome**
- Was a concrete next step secured with date confirmed?
- Or did the call end with vague "I'll think about it"?
- If a second demo with more stakeholders was requested — that is
  a positive signal, not a setback. Evaluate accordingly.

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

80–100 — MEDDIC 3+ confirmed (relative to stage) + real discovery
  with specific pains + prior context used + partner leveraged +
  concrete next step confirmed
50–79 — MEDDIC 2 confirmed + some discovery + next step agreed
  but vague OR partner not referenced
0–49 — MEDDIC 0–1 + generic pitch without discovery + no next step
  OR prior context completely ignored
