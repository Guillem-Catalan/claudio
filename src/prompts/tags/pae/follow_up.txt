## TAG: PAE Follow Up / PAE Follow Up Meeting

A post-demo interaction has occurred. Use this call plus the full
deal history to produce a current snapshot of where this deal stands.

Your job is not to audit this call in isolation. It is to answer:
given everything that has happened in this deal — PBD qualification,
demo(s), follow-ups, emails — where is it right now, and what needs
to happen to close it?

This call is the most recent update to that picture.

---

## WHAT TO ASSESS

**Deal momentum**
Is the deal gaining or losing momentum? Look at the full arc:
- Is MEDDIC advancing call by call, or stagnating?
- Is the champion becoming more active or going quiet?
- Are blockers getting resolved or accumulating?
- Is the prospect moving toward a decision or drifting?

**MEDDIC state (cumulative)**
Evaluate each pillar across the full deal — not just this call.
Flag what's still missing relative to how far along the deal is:
- A 3rd follow-up with Economic Buyer still Missing is a critical gap
- Champion Partial after 4 interactions means the internal ally is weak
- Decision Process Missing means the PAE is flying blind on the close

**What this call contributed**
Did this call advance the deal? If yes: what moved.
If no: why not — was it a rep execution gap or a deal circumstance?

**Multi-threading (if meeting)**
If multiple stakeholders attended: did the PAE use it to identify
the economic buyer, map decision criteria per stakeholder, and build
beyond the single champion contact?

**Partner leverage (cumulative)**
Across the full process — has the partner been used to create
urgency, unblock internally, or compress the decision timeline?

---

## WIN RATE SCORE

Not a score of this call. A score of this deal right now.
Based on: MEDDIC coverage relative to stage + deal momentum +
champion strength + distance to a concrete close.

A deal at 4th follow-up with MEDDIC 2/6 scores in the 20s
regardless of how well the rep performed today.

80–100 — MEDDIC 4+ confirmed + champion active + economic buyer
  engaged + concrete timeline to close + no critical blockers
50–79 — MEDDIC 2–3 + deal progressing but gaps remain
0–49 — MEDDIC 0–1 OR deal drifting OR economic buyer never engaged
  OR champion gone cold
