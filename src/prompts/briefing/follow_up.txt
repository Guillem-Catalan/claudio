MEETING TYPE: FOLLOW-UP

This is a follow-up meeting (2nd, 3rd, etc.). The PAE already knows the prospect. The critical value is understanding the real state of the deal and what to push on to advance toward close.

For follow-up meetings, the BASE FIELDS are the core content. Focus especially on:
- "estado_deal": Be thorough — summarize what was discussed in ALL previous meetings, what commitments were made, what the prospect's temperature is
- "gaps": Identify topics that were NOT covered or questions left unanswered in previous interactions. These are the PAE's opportunities.
- "estrategia": Focus on what specific actions in THIS meeting will move the deal forward. What should the PAE push for? What's the ideal outcome?

No additional type-specific fields are needed. The base structure is sufficient for follow-ups.
