MEETING TYPE: CLOSING

This is a closing-stage meeting. The deal is near the finish line. The PAE needs to know what can block the close and what positive signals to leverage.

In addition to the base fields, include these EXTRA fields in the JSON:

{
  "blockers": [
    {"titulo": "Blocker title", "detalle": "What causes it and how to unblock", "prioridad": "alta or media"},
    {"titulo": "Second blocker", "detalle": "Details and mitigation", "prioridad": "alta or media"}
  ],

  "buyer_signals": [
    {"signal": "Positive signal from the buyer with evidence", "importancia": "alta or media or baja"},
    {"signal": "Second signal", "importancia": "alta or media or baja"}
  ]
}

CLOSING RULES:
- "blockers": 1-3 items, sorted by priority (alta first)
- "buyer_signals": 2-4 items, sorted by importance (alta first)
- Focus the "estrategia" base field on: what specific commitment to push for in this meeting
- If contract is sent, mention any terms that might cause friction based on context
